Sources

Works cited and how they were checked

"Checked against text" means the wording was compared with the primary text: the LSB at read.lsbible.org, or the church fathers in the ANF and NPNF translations at New Advent. "Checked online" means the claim was confirmed against the source or publisher during this build. "Standard reference" means standard bibliographic details (title, date, position) that were not re-checked page by page. Verify page numbers before quoting them in print.

Scripture Checked against text

Legacy Standard Bible (LSB)

All Scripture quotations; every quoted phrase on this site was machine-checked against LSB text fetched from read.lsbible.org. Strong's numbers were checked against the LSB's own word tagging for the anchor verse.

Early church Checked against text

Didache 16

"the world-deceiver" appearing "as the Son of God" (Roberts-Donaldson translation, New Advent).

Early church Checked against text

Justin Martyr, Dialogue with Trypho 80-81

A thousand years in a rebuilt Jerusalem; admits that "many who belong to the pure and pious faith, and are true Christians, think otherwise" (ANF translation, New Advent).

Early church Checked against text

Irenaeus, Against Heresies 5.25-36

Antichrist (5.25); 666 and the names Evanthas, Lateinos, Teitan, and the copyists' 616 variant (5.30); Revelation seen "towards the end of Domitian's reign" (5.30.3); Papias' tradition of the vines (5.33.3); the kingdom (5.32-36) (ANF, New Advent).

Early church Checked against text

Augustine, City of God 20.7, 20.9, 20.19

Once held a millennial view (20.7); the saints reign with Christ now (20.9); on the restrainer, "I frankly confess I do not know what he means" (20.19, Dods translation, New Advent).
