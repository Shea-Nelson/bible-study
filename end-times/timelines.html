Daniel 9:24-27

Revelation's numbers of time

  • 1,260 days (11:3; 12:6) = 42 months (11:2; 13:5) = time, times, and half a time (12:14; Dan 7:25; 12:7): 3½ years, half of seven. Futurists take it literally. Others see the limited, cut-short time of the church's suffering.
  • Five months (9:5, 10): the locusts' torment, about the life-span of a locust season.
  • Hour, day, month, and year (9:15): God's exact timing.
  • One hour (17:12; 18:10, 17, 19): the brevity of the ten kings' power and Babylon's sudden fall.
  • Three and a half days (11:9, 11): the two witnesses lie dead.
  • A thousand years (20:2-7): see the views.
